# Morning Light

Soft gold slips across the sill,
a hush of dust in quiet air—
the day begins, unhurried, still,
as if the world has time to spare.

Outside, a sparrow finds its song,
a thin bright thread against the blue;
the coffee steams, the night feels long
behind us, finished, almost through.

We hold this hour like a cup,
warm and ordinary, enough—
and for a moment, looking up,
the ordinary feels like love.
