How to Play

  1. Press Start to begin a new game.
  2. Read the English concept shown in the Prompt, or press Listen to hear the Spanish word pronounced.
  3. Find the matching Spanish word before it falls off the bottom of the Game Area.
  4. Each correct match earns points and grows your Streak.
  5. Each missed or wrong word costs one of your Lives (hearts). Lose all three and the game is over.
