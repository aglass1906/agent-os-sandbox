How to Play Connect Four

Objective

Be the first player to get four of your discs in a row.

The Grid
  1. The game is played on a standing 6 × 7 board: 6 rows of slots above 7 columns.
  2. Each slot holds one of three values: empty, a Red disc, or a Yellow disc.
  3. Red always goes first, then turns strictly alternate.
Dropping Discs
  1. Click any slot in a column to drop your disc into that column.
  2. The disc falls to the lowest open slot in the column.
  3. A column that is full of 6 discs cannot be played into.
  4. Once a disc is placed it cannot be moved.
Winning
  1. You win by placing four of your discs in a continuous line.
  2. Winning lines run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally (both directions).
  3. The win is checked immediately after each drop, so the game ends as soon as a line of four is completed.
Draw
  1. If all 42 slots are filled and no player has four in a row, the game is a draw.
  2. A win is always reported in preference to a draw for the same final board.
  3. Press New Game to reset the board and start fresh at any time.
Game States

The game is always in one of four states: Playing (a turn is in progress), Player Red wins, Player Yellow wins, or Draw. The status bar above the board shows the current state and whose turn it is.
