HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Be the first to place four of your discs in a continuous line — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

The Grid
  1. The board is a standing 6 × 7 grid: 6 rows of slots above 7 columns.
  2. Each slot holds one of three values: empty, a Red disc, or a Yellow disc.
  3. Red always goes first, then turns strictly alternate.
How to Play
  1. Click any slot in a column to drop your disc into that column.
  2. The disc falls to the lowest open slot in the column, stacking on any discs already there.
  3. A column already holding 6 discs is full and cannot be played into.
  4. Once a disc is placed, it can never be moved.
Feedback
  • R Red discs belong to the first player.
  • Y Yellow discs belong to the second player.
  • 4 Flashing slots mark the four winning discs when a player wins.
Draw
  1. If all 42 slots fill up with no four-in-a-row, the game is a draw.
  2. A win is always reported in preference to a draw on the same final board.
