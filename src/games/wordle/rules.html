HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Guess the hidden 5-letter word within 6 attempts. After every guess, the tiles will show how close you are to the answer.

Rules
  1. Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  2. You have 6 attempts to find the answer word.
  3. Type your guess with the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard, then press Enter to submit it.
  4. Use Backspace to delete a letter before submitting.
  5. After each guess, every letter you played is colored to reveal how it relates to the answer.
  6. Guess the word to win. If all 6 attempts are used without a match, the game is over.
Feedback
  • C Green means the letter is in the answer and in the correct spot.
  • R Yellow means the letter is in the answer but in the wrong spot.
  • A Gray means the letter is not in the answer at all.
Example
C R A N E

If the answer were CRANE, the C is in the right place, the R appears somewhere else in the word, and A, N, and E are not in the answer.
