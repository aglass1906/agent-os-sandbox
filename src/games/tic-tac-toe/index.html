How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Objective

Be the first player to get three of your markers in a row.

The Grid
  1. The game is played on a standard 3 × 3 board of nine cells.
  2. Each cell holds one of three values: empty, X, or O.
  3. A move is legal only in an empty cell. Legal moves exist in every non-terminal state.
Taking Turns
  1. Two players share the board: one plays X, the other plays O.
  2. Player X always goes first, then turns strictly alternate.
  3. Click an empty cell to place your marker. Once a cell is filled, it cannot be changed.
  4. After a win or draw the game is over and no further moves are accepted.
Winning
  1. You win by placing three of your markers in a continuous line.
  2. There are 8 possible winning lines: 3 horizontal, 3 vertical, and 2 diagonal.
  3. The win is checked immediately after each move, so the game ends as soon as a line of three is completed.
Draw
  1. If all nine cells are filled and no player has three in a row, the game is a draw.
  2. A win is always reported in preference to a draw for the same final board.
  3. Press New Game to reset the board and start fresh at any time.
Game States

The game is always in one of four states: Playing (a turn is in progress), Player X wins, Player O wins, or Draw. The status bar above the board shows the current state and whose turn it is.
