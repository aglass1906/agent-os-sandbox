Objective

Fill the 9 × 9 grid so that every row, every column, and every 3 × 3 box contains the digits 1-9 exactly once.

The Grid
  1. The board has 9 rows and 9 columns, divided into nine 3×3 boxes.
  2. Some cells are pre-filled at the start; they are locked and cannot be changed.
  3. Click an empty cell to select it, then enter a digit 1-9.
How to Play
  1. Select a cell with a mouse click or the arrow keys.
  2. Type a digit 1-9 with your keyboard or tap it on the number pad.
  3. Press Backspace or Delete (or the ⌫ button) to clear the selected cell.
  4. A digit is illegal if it would appear twice in the same row, column, or 3×3 box; the conflict is highlighted in red.
  5. Once every cell is filled correctly, the puzzle is solved and the game is won.
Feedback
  • 3 No highlight means the digit is rule-clean.
  • 3 Red means the digit already appears in its row, column, or box.
  • 3 Bold means the cell is part of the starting puzzle and locked.
