How to Play Simon

Objective

Repeat an ever-growing sequence of color flashes to score as many levels as you can.

The Board
  1. The board displays four colored pads arranged in a square: Green, Red, Yellow, and Blue.
  2. Each pad has a keyboard shortcut: 1/Q Green, 2/W Red, 3/A Yellow, 4/S Blue.
Playing
  1. Press Start to begin a new game.
  2. Watch the sequence of pads that flash during the computer's turn.
  3. Repeat the exact sequence by clicking the pads or pressing their keyboard shortcuts.
  4. If you repeat the sequence correctly, a new color is added and the sequence replays.
  5. If you make a mistake, the game is over.
Scoring
  1. Your level equals the number of sequences you have completed.
  2. Your score is calculated from the levels reached.
  3. The best score is saved for the duration of the browser session.
  4. Press Start at any time to restart the game.
Game States

The game cycles through these states: Idle (waiting to start), Demo (pads are flashing the sequence), Player (your turn to repeat the sequence), Won (sequence completed), and Game Over (mistake made). The status bar shows the current state.
