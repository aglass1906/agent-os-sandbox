How to Play Memory Match

Objective

Flip over cards to find every matching pair on the board.

The Board
  1. The board holds a grid of face-down cards, each showing a hidden symbol.
  2. Every symbol appears on exactly two cards, forming a matching pair.
Playing
  1. Click or tap a card to flip it face up and reveal its symbol.
  2. Click or tap a second card to try to find its match.
  3. If the two cards match, they stay face up. If not, they flip face down again.
  4. The game ends when every pair has been matched.
Winning
  1. Match all of the pairs on the board to win.
  2. Your score tracks the number of pairs matched, the moves taken, and the elapsed time.
  3. Press New Game to shuffle the cards and start fresh at any time.
