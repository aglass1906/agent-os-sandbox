How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Objective

Be the first player to get three of your markers in a row.

Rules
  1. The game is played on a 3 × 3 grid.
  2. Players take turns placing their own marker; one player uses X and the other uses O.
  3. Player X always goes first, then turns alternate between players.
  4. Click an empty cell to place your marker. Once a cell is filled, it cannot be changed.
  5. The first player to line up three of their markers horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins.
  6. If all nine cells are filled with no three in a row, the game is a draw.
